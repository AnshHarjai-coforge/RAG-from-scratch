# Remote Work Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This document describes Northstar Analytics' remote-work rules as they applied beginning April 1, 2024. It is retained in the policy archive for historical reference. It applies to regular full-time and part-time employees in the United States. Contractors and interns should follow their agreements and any additional instructions provided by their business sponsor.

Remote work is a flexible work arrangement. It does not change an employee's responsibilities, expected working hours, performance standards, or obligation to attend important in-person meetings. Managers may ask employees to attend the office when customer needs, training, collaboration, or operational requirements make in-person work necessary.

## 2. Standard remote-work arrangement

Under this version of the policy, eligible employees may work remotely for up to **three days per week**. Employees are expected to work from a Northstar office for the remaining workdays unless their manager approves a different schedule. Teams may establish shared office days to support collaboration.

Employees seeking a recurring arrangement must submit a request to their manager. The request should state the proposed remote days, primary work location, requested start date, and any anticipated effect on team coverage. **Written approval from the employee's manager is sufficient** to begin the arrangement. People Operations may be consulted by the manager when the request raises eligibility, accommodation, or work-location questions. Employees should retain the manager's approval for their records.

Managers may approve occasional remote days for practical reasons, such as a home repair appointment or a short-term transportation disruption. Repeated requests should be discussed as a recurring arrangement so that team coverage remains predictable.

## 3. Eligibility and performance expectations

Remote work may be approved when the employee's duties can be performed effectively away from a Northstar office. Roles that require physical records, specialized equipment, secure facilities, or regular on-site customer support may have more limited flexibility. Managers should consider the employee's performance, role requirements, and the needs of the team.

Employees working remotely must remain reachable during their normal working hours, attend scheduled meetings, maintain an accurate calendar, and meet established deadlines. Employees should notify their manager promptly if an internet outage, workspace issue, or other disruption prevents them from working. Remote work should not be used in place of sick leave when an employee is unable to perform their duties.

## 4. Work location and equipment

Employees must identify their normal remote work location in their request. The location should be safe, suitable for focused work, and sufficiently private for business conversations. Employees who want to work from another state or country must obtain written approval from their manager before doing so. The manager should consult People Operations if the change is expected to last more than ten business days.

Northstar may provide a laptop, monitor, headset, or other equipment based on role requirements. Company equipment remains company property and must be returned when requested or when employment ends. Employees are responsible for maintaining a reliable internet connection and a workspace that allows them to work safely.

## 5. Security and confidentiality

Employees must protect company information wherever they work. Company devices must be password-protected and locked when unattended. Employees should use approved applications and secure network access, avoid discussing confidential matters where others can overhear, and prevent family members or visitors from viewing company information.

Public Wi-Fi should be used only with the company's approved secure access method. Employees should avoid printing confidential documents at home. Any lost device, suspected unauthorized access, or accidental disclosure must be reported to IT Security promptly.

## 6. Changes and exceptions

Managers may review an arrangement if business requirements, employee responsibilities, performance, or team coverage change. A manager may modify or end an arrangement after discussing the reason with the employee and providing reasonable notice where practicable. Requests for disability-related accommodations or other legally protected arrangements should be referred to People Operations for an individualized review.

## 7. Policy administration

People Operations owns this policy and may issue guidance to support consistent application. This version was effective April 1, 2024 and was superseded by version 3.0 effective January 1, 2026. For current requirements, employees should consult the current Remote Work Policy. Historical questions may be sent to peopleops@northstaranalytics.example.
