# Paid Time Off Policy

## 1. Purpose and eligibility

Northstar Analytics provides paid time off (PTO) so employees can rest, manage personal matters, and take planned vacations. This policy applies to regular full-time US employees. Part-time employees, temporary workers, interns, and employees outside the United States receive leave according to their offer terms, applicable local law, or a separate written policy.

PTO is intended to provide flexibility while ensuring teams can meet customer and operational commitments. Employees should plan leave with their manager, provide reasonable notice, and document approved time away in the HR system and shared team calendar. Nothing in this policy limits statutory leave or legally protected rights.

## 2. Annual PTO allowance and accrual

Eligible full-time employees receive **20 days (160 hours) of PTO per calendar year**. PTO accrues each pay period at a rate equivalent to the annual allowance. Employees hired during the year receive a prorated allowance based on their start date. The HR system displays each employee's available balance and is the official record for planning and payroll purposes.

PTO may be used in increments of one hour, subject to system availability and manager approval. Employees should not schedule more PTO than their available balance unless People Operations has approved an exception in writing. Negative PTO balances are not normally permitted. If an employee has an approved negative balance, the amount may be recovered from future accruals or handled at separation as permitted by applicable law.

## 3. Requesting and approving PTO

For planned leave of **three or more consecutive business days**, employees should submit a request at least two weeks in advance. For one or two days, at least five business days' notice is preferred. Managers should respond within three business days when practical. Requests should identify the dates, total hours, and any coverage arrangements for time-sensitive work.

Approval depends on available balance, team coverage, customer commitments, and competing requests. Managers should apply these factors consistently and avoid treating an employee's use of accrued PTO negatively. A request is not approved until the HR system shows approval or the manager confirms approval in writing. Employees should not make non-refundable travel commitments before approval.

For an unexpected illness or urgent personal matter, employees should notify their manager as soon as possible, preferably before their normal start time. The employee or manager should enter the absence in the HR system when practical. The manager may ask for an estimated return date to support coverage planning, but employees are not required to disclose a diagnosis.

## 4. Carryover and year-end treatment

Employees may carry over up to **40 hours** of unused PTO into the next calendar year. Hours above the carryover limit are forfeited at year-end unless a different treatment is required by applicable law or approved in writing by People Operations. Employees should review their balances in the fourth quarter and coordinate planned leave early enough to avoid losing hours.

PTO is not intended to replace statutory sick leave, family and medical leave, disability leave, jury duty, bereavement leave, or other protected leave where a separate entitlement applies. Where required by law, Northstar will apply the more protective rule. People Operations can explain how different leave categories interact.

## 5. Holidays and other leave categories

Company holidays are published annually and are separate from the 20-day PTO allowance. If a company holiday falls during an approved PTO period, the holiday is not deducted from the employee's PTO balance. Employees required to work on a company holiday should consult their manager and People Operations about the applicable schedule and compensation rules.

Bereavement, jury duty, military leave, parental leave, and legally protected medical or family leave are administered under applicable law and any relevant Northstar policy. Employees should contact People Operations to confirm the correct leave category rather than automatically entering these absences as PTO.

## 6. Separation from employment

At separation, unused accrued PTO will be paid out where required by the law applicable to the employee's work location. In locations where payout is not legally required, Northstar's default practice is to pay the unused accrued balance, subject to any lawful written agreement or policy exception. The final balance is calculated from the HR system and reconciled by Payroll. Employees should return company property and complete handover responsibilities before their last day.

## 7. Responsibilities and policy administration

Employees are responsible for submitting accurate requests and recording leave promptly. Managers are responsible for reviewing requests consistently, arranging coverage, and ensuring approved absences are reflected in team plans. People Operations maintains the HR system, interprets this policy, and reviews it annually. Questions should be sent to peopleops@northstaranalytics.example.
