# Remote Work Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains when employees of Northstar Analytics may work remotely, how remote arrangements are approved, and what employees must do to protect company information while working away from a Northstar office. It applies to regular full-time and part-time employees in the United States. Contractors, interns, and employees working outside the United States must follow the terms of their agreement and any location-specific guidance supplied by People Operations.

Remote work is a work-location arrangement, not a change to an employee's role, expected results, working hours, or responsibility to remain available to colleagues and customers. A manager may require office attendance for a particular meeting, customer engagement, training session, or business need, subject to applicable accommodation requirements and law.

## 2. Standard remote-work arrangement

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to **two days per week**. The employee and manager should agree on a predictable weekly schedule and record it in the team's shared calendar. The remaining workdays are normally office-based. Teams may set common in-office days to support collaboration, provided those expectations are communicated in advance.

An employee who wants a recurring arrangement must submit a request to their manager and People Operations. The request should identify the proposed remote days, the employee's primary work location, the expected start date, and any relevant operational considerations. The arrangement begins only after **both the manager and People Operations approve it in writing**. Approval is normally issued within five business days.

A manager may approve occasional one-off remote days for practical reasons, such as a home repair appointment, when team coverage is maintained. Repeated one-off requests should be handled as a recurring arrangement. Employees must not assume that a request has been approved merely because it was discussed verbally.

## 3. Eligibility and performance expectations

Remote work is available when the role can be performed effectively away from a Northstar office and the employee has demonstrated reliable performance. Some duties involving physical records, specialized equipment, secure facilities, or on-site customer support may require more frequent attendance. Managers should apply eligibility criteria consistently and consult People Operations when uncertain.

Remote employees must attend scheduled meetings, keep their calendars accurate, respond through agreed communication channels during normal working hours, and meet the same deadlines and quality standards that apply in the office. Employees should tell their manager promptly if a connectivity, caregiving, safety, or workspace issue prevents them from working as planned. Remote work is not a substitute for sick leave or dependent-care leave when an employee is unable to work.

## 4. Work location, equipment, and expenses

The approved remote location must be within the employee's authorized employment location and suitable for confidential work. Employees may not routinely work from another state or country without written approval from People Operations and Payroll. A change in work location can affect tax, insurance, employment, and data-protection obligations.

Northstar may provide a laptop, monitor, headset, and other equipment based on role requirements. Company equipment remains company property and must be returned when requested or when employment ends. Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe, reasonably private workspace and a reliable internet connection. Reimbursement for eligible work expenses is governed by the Expense Reimbursement Policy; this policy does not create a separate reimbursement entitlement.

## 5. Security and confidentiality

Employees must use company-approved devices, applications, and network access methods. Devices must be protected with a strong password or approved authentication method, automatic screen locking, and current security updates. Employees must not allow family members or other people to use company devices, view confidential information, or listen to private calls.

When using public Wi-Fi, employees must connect through the company-approved secure access method. Confidential documents should not be printed at home unless the manager has approved the business need and the employee can store and dispose of the pages securely. Suspected loss of a device, unauthorized access, or accidental disclosure must be reported to IT Security immediately.

## 6. Changes, review, and exceptions

Managers may review an arrangement when business needs, role responsibilities, performance, or team coverage changes. A material change to recurring remote days requires renewed written approval from the manager and People Operations. Northstar may suspend or modify an arrangement with reasonable notice where practicable. Employees may request an exception for a disability-related accommodation or other legally protected reason through People Operations; such requests will be evaluated individually and confidentially.

People Operations owns this policy and reviews it at least annually. Questions should be sent to peopleops@northstaranalytics.example.
